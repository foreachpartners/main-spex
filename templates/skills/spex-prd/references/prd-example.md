# Product requirements

## Problem

Shoppers forget what to buy when they reach the store. A paper list gets lost or left at home, and a general notes app is slow to scan with a cart in hand.

## Goal

A shopping list that is quick to fill in at home and quick to use in the store, so the shopper leaves with everything they planned to buy.

## Users

- **Shopper** — keeps a personal list and checks items off while in the store.

## Success

- A shopper tells at a glance what is still left to buy.

## Capabilities

- **Add item** — the shopper adds an item to the list.
- **View list** — the shopper sees every item and which are bought.
- **Mark bought** — the shopper marks an item as bought.
- **Delete item** — the shopper removes an item from the list.
- **Clear list** — the shopper removes all items at once.

## Non-goals

- **Accounts and shared lists** — the list belongs to one shopper.
- **Quantities, prices, and categories** — an item is a name.
